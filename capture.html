Record training data

Records a signer's letters and signs so STAT's models can learn from real signers. Recording takes about 20–30 minutes.

This page saves only hand and body landmark positions (dots), not video or photos. They will be used to train and test STAT's sign models, kept in the project's private storage, and never posted publicly. If they are used to train STAT, the project will say so.
