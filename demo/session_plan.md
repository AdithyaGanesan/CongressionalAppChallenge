# Recording session checklist (capture page)

Open `https://adithyaganesan.github.io/CongressionalAppChallenge/capture.html` on the phone
(or tap "Record training data" on STAT's start screen).

## Before starting
- [ ] Ask the signer for consent. Show them the consent box on screen; tick it only if they agree.
- [ ] Signer ID: `S1` for the actor, `S2` for the ASL professional, and so on. Set the signing hand.
- [ ] Prop the phone so the signer's head, shoulders and hips are in frame, in good light.
- [ ] Charge the phone. The page keeps the screen awake while it's open.

## Letters A–Z: 3 rounds (highest priority, about 10 minutes)
- [ ] Round 1: tap A, then Record. Hold the letter through the 3-second countdown and 5 seconds of recording. The page moves on to the next letter by itself.
- [ ] Round 2: same, with the hand a little closer to or farther from the phone.
- [ ] Round 3: same, turned slightly left or right.
- A letter's button turns green at 3 takes. J and Z move: sign the motion during the 5 seconds.
- "Not saved: only N frames" means the hand wasn't seen. Check the framing and redo it.

## Signs: 10 takes each (about 10 minutes)
- [ ] TODAY, UNDERSTAND, GOOD, GREAT, HELLO, THANK-YOU
- Record, then sign once with hands up, then lower the hands. The take saves when the hands come down.

## Phrases (test data, not for training): 5 each if there's time
- [ ] Phrase tab: type the label exactly, e.g. `TODAY C-A-L-I-B-R-A-T-E`, then Record and perform it.
- [ ] Each fingerspelled script word (CALIBRATE, SENSORS) as its own phrase.

## After
- [ ] Tap **Save zip**, then on iPhone choose "Save to Files" or Google Drive. Takes also stay on the phone as a backup until you delete them.
- [ ] Put the zip in your private Google Drive. **Never commit it to GitHub.**
- [ ] If the actor's takes are used for training, the video or submission must say so, and test numbers must come from takes that weren't trained on.
